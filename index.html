Welcome

Look. Talk. Sent.

saywhen types what you say into your terminal or coding assistant. No buttons, no hotkeys. It only listens while you are looking at the screen and talking.

  1. 01Look at the screen.
  2. 02Talk like you would to a person.
  3. 03Stop. Check the text, and it is sent.
Pick how you want to send. You can change it any time on Home.
