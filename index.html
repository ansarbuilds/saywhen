Talk to your computer like a colleague

saywhen types what you say into your terminal or coding assistant. Look at the screen, talk, stop. It shows you the text for a moment, then sends it.

1 Get started in three steps

  1. Turn on the camera on Home. Pictures are checked on this computer and never saved.
  2. Look at the screen you talk to, keep your mouth closed and click Calibrate. Do this again if you move your laptop or camera.
  3. Talk. When you stop, saywhen shows your words, counts down and sends them. Say “cancel”, shake your head or press Esc to stop it.

2 Choose how you send

Just pauseStop talking and it sends after a short countdown. Best for quick commands.
Nod to sendNothing goes out until you nod. Best when you think out loud.
Say “send it”Enter is pressed only when you end with “send it”.
Type onlyYour words are typed and you press Enter yourself. The safest way to start.

Do These work well

  • Speak in whole sentences, then stop for a moment.
  • Say commands the way you would type them: “git status”, “cargo test dash dash release”.
  • Calibrate again if it says “I can see you, but you look away”.
  • Start with “Eevee” when you are not facing the camera, if the wake word is on.
  • Add your project names under Settings → Words to always spell right.

Don’t Avoid these

  • Don’t expect risky commands like rm -rf to send by themselves. They are only typed, on purpose.
  • Don’t switch windows during the countdown. saywhen stops instead of typing into the wrong place.
  • Don’t pick a common wake word like “okay”. It will start by mistake.
  • Don’t start saywhen from an AI assistant’s shell on a Mac. The camera may be blocked. Use Terminal.

3 Ways to stop or fix a send

Need an action fast? Press ⌘ K on a Mac or Ctrl K on Windows, or click Commands in the sidebar.

4 When something goes wrong

It never starts listening
Check the camera is on and click Calibrate while looking at your screen. Or turn on the wake word in Settings.
Nothing gets typed
On a Mac, allow saywhen under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
It typed but did not press Enter
The app may not be on the safe list in Settings, or the command looked risky.
It starts when I do not want it to
Turn up the noise filter in Settings, or pick Nod to send.
Something else
Read the Problems list on Home. You can copy the message and open an issue on GitHub.
