# AI coding assistant and coding agent adoption

Research date: 12 November 2026. Figures below are copied from primary survey or platform reports. Surveys are not averaged. A vendor user count is not treated as a share of all developers. "Vibe coder," meaning someone who builds software mainly by directing a coding agent, including non-engineers, is not a category in any survey retrieved. Where a source says only "AI tool" and does not name coding, that figure is not relabeled as a coding-assistant share.

## Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2024, 2025, and 2026

### Takeaway

Stack Overflow's own surveys show AI in the development workflow moving from 62% currently using in 2024 to about 79% currently using in 2025 (84% using or planning). The 2026 survey, now published, separates coding assistants from general chat: 65.9% of people who answered said they use an AI coding assistant or coding agent at work, and 17.2% said they use no AI tools. Named-product shares are usually a subset of respondents, not all developers, and "use" is published separately from "plan" or "want."

### Inferences

- The 2024 and 2025 headlines mix "using" with "planning." The usable current-use figures are 62% in 2024 and about 79% in 2025, with 5.3% in 2025 saying they plan to use soon and 16.2% saying they do not plan to.
- Stack Overflow's 2025 Claude Code figures describe different bases: about 9.7% as an editor in the survey tooling list, versus 41% among the 8,323 people on the out-of-the-box agent list. The 2026 figure, 65.5%, is among 12,255 people who answered the product question.
- The 2026 coding-assistant share (65.9% of 17,464) can sit next to a high agent-product share inside the product question because the product question is a smaller, overlapping group.

### Gaps

- 2024 professional-developer-only percentage for "currently use AI tools" was not in the page text. The chart exists, but the text publishes the all-respondent 62% and 76%.
- 2024 response count for the ChatGPT 82% / Copilot 41% / Gemini 24% chart was not in the fetch.
- 2025 exact answer count attached to the 84% headline was not unambiguously labeled.
- 2026 "want to use in the next 6 months" percentages for Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, and the other named agents were not in the extract, even though the question asks them.
- 2026 methodology extract does not say whether results were weighted, or whether non-professional respondents were removed.
- Codeium, under that name, was not found as a 2024, 2025, or 2026 annual-survey share. Windsurf appears in 2025 (4.9%) and 2026 (6.5% of the product-question respondents).
- Cursor was not found in the 2024 top-three tool list.

## GitHub Octoverse 2024, 2025, and 2026

### Takeaway

Octoverse is GitHub reporting on GitHub. The figures are counts of accounts, repositories, pull requests, and Copilot users. They are not a survey share of all developers. The closest survey-style figure in the 2024 report is 73% of open source respondents using AI tools such as Copilot for coding or documentation. In 2026, more than 40% of GitHub pull requests in September started from an agent's first draft, and 55% of Copilot's monthly active users used agentic features.

### Cited Findings
