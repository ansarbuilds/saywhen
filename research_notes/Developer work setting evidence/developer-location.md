# Developer work location, 2023 through 2026

Scope note for the report writer: these notes cover developer surveys only. Stack Overflow and JetBrains are industry surveys with their own self-selected samples. They are not probability samples of all professional developers, and they should not be averaged together. Survey year and fieldwork year are both recorded when the source states them. Pages were opened on 12 November 2026.

Every statistic below uses the same fields. If a field was not published, it says "not found".

## Stack Overflow remote, hybrid, and in-person shares, 2023–2026

### Takeaway

Stack Overflow asks "Which best describes your current work situation?" The published charts are for all respondents who answered, not a professional-developers-only cut. In 2023 and 2024 the answers were three categories. In 2025 and 2026 the same question used five answers: fully remote, two kinds of hybrid, in-person, and a flexible "your choice." Among all respondents, remote was 41.41% in 2023 and 38% in 2024, then 32.38% in 2025 and 34.8% in 2026 under the new answer list. In-person was 16.41% in 2023, 20% in 2024, 17.88% in 2025, and 17.6% in 2026.

### Inferences

- The 2023 and 2024 instruments can be compared with each other. The 2025 and 2026 instruments can be compared with each other. Comparing a 2024 "Remote" share with a 2025 "Remote" share mixes two different choice sets, because 2025 added "Your choice" and split hybrid into two answers.
